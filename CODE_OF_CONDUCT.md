# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Rebax is a project for everyone who wants to build with it or help build it. We expect everyone who takes part in the repositories, discussions and other project spaces to:

- Be respectful and patient with other people.
- Give and accept feedback about work, not about the person.
- Assume good intent and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful.

The following is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about any person or group.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, advertising unrelated to the project, or deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently block people who repeatedly or seriously break it.

To report a problem, use the private contact method described in `SECURITY.md`, or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.
